Frequently Asked Questions

01

Why use Spots to curate & plan my trip?

We provide access to hidden gems, personalized itineraries based on deep local knowledge, and handle all logistics so you can simply focus on experiencing the journey.

How does working with you work?

It starts with a conversation to understand your travel style. From there, we present an initial proposal, refine it collaboratively, and then manage all bookings and details until you return home.

How pricing works

We charge a flat planning fee based on the complexity and length of your trip, ensuring transparent pricing with no hidden markups on the experiences we book for you.
